Your local mechanic on Archers Road

Servicing and repairs for every make and model

Top Motor Service has been looking after North Shore cars from our Archers Road workshop since 2011. Whether you need a WOF, a regular service or a repair, we check your car carefully and tell you honestly what it needs.

The workshop is run by Alan and his team. Many customers have been coming back to Alan for years, and most new ones find us through friends and family.

Book a service

How booking works

Book online Monday to Friday, at least one day ahead. Pick the option that suits your day, and tell us about your car in the booking form.

Wait while we work

Book a time and wait at the workshop. Good for WOFs and lube services, which usually take 30 to 40 minutes.

Drop off and pick up

Drop your car off in the morning and tell us when you need it back, for example drop off before 10am and collect at 2pm.

Courtesy car

Need to keep moving? We have 5 courtesy cars. Tell us how long you need one for, and we'll have your car done in that time. Book ahead, as they go quickly.
