Top Motor Service

Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

Top Motor Service Ltd ("we", "us") is a car repair and WOF workshop at 3/207 Archers Road, Glenfield, Auckland. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it and how we look after it, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your vehicle's number plate, make and model
  • Details about the work you need and the work we do on your vehicle
  • Booking details, such as the time you choose and whether you'll wait, drop off or need a courtesy car
  • For courtesy cars, your driver licence details and the kilometres driven

Why we collect it

  • To book your vehicle in and contact you about it
  • To carry out WOF inspections, servicing and repairs
  • To order parts and issue invoices
  • To lend and charge for courtesy cars

We only use your information for these purposes. We don't sell it or share it for marketing.

Online bookings and Google

Our online booking page uses Google Calendar appointment schedules. When you book online, the details you enter are sent to and stored by Google, which may hold them on servers outside New Zealand. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. If you'd rather not book online, you can call or text us on 021 893 421 instead.

Who we share it with

We may share the details needed with parts suppliers, or where the law requires it, for example vehicle inspection records required for WOF certification. We don't share your information with anyone else without your permission.

How we keep it safe

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it's wrong. Contact us:

  • Phone or text: 021 893 421
  • Email: topmotorservice@hotmail.com
  • In person: 3/207 Archers Road, Glenfield, Auckland 0629

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
